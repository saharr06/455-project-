EASY GUIDE

How to use the website.

You don't need to know any technical terms. Just follow these steps.

1 — HIDE SOMETHING

Choose audio or video

Pick a WAV audio file or a short video. This is the file that will hold your secret.

2 — ADD YOUR SECRET

Write a message or choose a file

You can hide words, a picture, a PDF or another small file. Add a password if you want.

3 — SAVE IT

Download your new file

Click “Hide my secret” and wait until the new audio or video is ready. Keep the downloaded file.

4 — OPEN THE SECRET

Use “Find a hidden message”

Choose the new file, enter the password if one was used, and see your message or download your hidden file.

Tips for a quick test

Start with a short WAV audio file and a simple message like “Hello!”. Video can take longer, so try a 3–5 second clip first.

Important: Changing or compressing the finished audio or video may damage the hidden secret. Share the file without changing it.
